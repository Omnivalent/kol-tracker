How to read it

What counts

SOL, USDC and USDT moved directly between a KOL's wallet and another real wallet, $500 or more. Private counterparties are checked one ring further out.

What doesn't

Any transaction that touches a DEX, AMM, bonding curve, liquidity pool or other program. Pool and vault accounts never appear. Exchanges and bots are marked, not followed.

How it grows

A scan runs every six hours: it reads new activity, then reaches further back in time. Links are leads, not proof; open the transactions to judge each one.
